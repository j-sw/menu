Minced pork with egg

Ingredients

300g ground pork (30% fat)
2 eggs
3 cloves garlic
thumb of ginger
2 green onions
1 tbsp oil
SAUCE
2 tbsp dark soy
1 tbsp light soy
1 tbsp oyster sauce
1 tsp sugar
1 tsp sesame oil
1 tbsp cornstarch
1 cup water
white pepper
sichuan pepper (optional)

Directions

combine sauce
in a wok, heat oil & cook mince until crisp / brown
add garlic, ginger, white part onion, fry until aromatic
pour sauce around edges, crack eggs in centre and cover for ~ 2 mins
serve with rice & green parts of onion for garnish
