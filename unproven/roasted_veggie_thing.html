Roasted veggie thing

Ingredients

large veggie (eggplant / tomato / zucchini)
carrot, chopped
onion, chopped
celery, chopped
garlic, chopped
peppers
mushrooms, thinly sliced
pine nuts
olive oil
sesame oil
salt / pepper

Directions

LARGE VEGGIE
slice large veggie in half, cut exposed flesh like a mango
drizzle with olive oil, salt, in the oven (grill 180) until crispy (flip a couple of times)
separate off the roasted flesh leaving the skins
PEPPERS
grill the peppers and chop
MUSHROOMS
in a pan, melt butter & olive oil, add thinly sliced mushrooms, garlic, a dash of white wine, cover
once mushrooms have softened / lost their water, uncover and fry until mushrooms are crispy / bacony
MIREPOIX
fry carrot, onion, celery, garlic, until softened / fragrant
PINE NUTS
gently fry in olive oil & sesame oil
FINAL
combine large veggie flesh, peppers, mushrooms, mirepoix, pine nuts
add mix to veggie skins
grill
enjoy!
