Marjorie's Carrot Cake

As written:

Mix
2 eggs,
1 cup (284 ml) caster sugar,
3/4 cup (213 ml) of peanut oil,
1 teaspoon vanilla essence,
1 cup (284 ml) plain flour,
1 teaspoon bicarbonate of soda,
.5 teaspoon of salt,
1 teaspoon of mixed spice (+ cinnamon if you want)
- sieve dry stuff

Bash it all up. Mixer is ok.

add
1.5 cups (426ml) finely grated carrot
.5 cup (142ml) chopped nuts (almonds are good! but can also do walnuts)
mix all

(bash the nuts up very finely, put them in a teatowel or something and whack with something heavy. Takes a while, but you don't want them too chunky)

Bake in a moderate oven 45-55 mins (say 180-200 degrees)

Cooked when the cake has detached from the side of the tin
(remember to grease the tin with butter first, or the cake will stick)
(can also check by inserting a knife / skewer, if it comes out dry it's done)

Icing:
2 ozs (56.7 grams) softened unsalted butter,
2 ozs (56.7 grams) philadelphia cheese,
1 cup (284 ml) of sifted icing sugar,
grated lemon rind,
1 egg yolk
mix together & add lemon juice till tart (i.e. to taste)

(To spread the icing, get a bowl of boiling water, and dip a knife in it - this will stop the icing sticking to the knife. Once you've evenly coated the cake, put it in the fridge to let the icing set. Oh, and let the cake cool in the air first)

Note: If you don't cool the cake first the icing will melt and penetrate the cake, making it sweet and lemony... JG
