Tarta de Santiago

Ingredients

for four people:
- 250 g of ground almond
- 170 g of sugar (the original recipe says 250, but we usually put just 170 or similar)
- Grated lemon rind (from an average lemon, don't put too much or it will only taste of it)
- 3 eggs
- A stream of cognac (or brandy if you don't have cognac)
- A tablespoonful of baking powder
- Butter
- Flour (in case of gluten intolerance sugar works fine instead, it's just for lining the mould)
- Powdered sugar

Directions

- Preheat the oven to 200 ºC
- Mix the eggs with the sugar, then the ground almond, the grated lemon rind, the cognac and the baking powder
- Butter and flour the cake pan (grease the pan with butter and then coat with flour)
- Bake it at 200ºC the first 5 minutes, and then at 180ºC the next 20 minutes
- Remove it from its mould. and sprinkle powdered sugar all over
