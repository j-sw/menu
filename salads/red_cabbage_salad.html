Red cabbage salad

Ingredients

1/2 red cabbage
spring onions
pine nuts or sesame seeds
sesame oil
vinaigrette (olive oil, 1/2 as much balsamic, salt, pepper)

Directions

thinly slice the red cabbage
blanch in a pot of boiling water (it should soften slightly, but not cook through / go limp) and drain
thinly slice spring onions, set aside some for garnish, mix the rest through
add vinaigrette
in a small pan, heat sesame oil and gently fry the pine nuts or sesame seeds (you can do both, but do them separately as they need different times)
once golden, set aside some for garnish, pour the rest over the salad and mix through
garnish with remaining onions and toasted seeds (should be a festive purple, golden, and green)
