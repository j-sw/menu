Endive and apple salad

Ingredients

2-3 endives
1/2 red apple
vinaigrette (olive oil, half as much balsamic, salt, pepper)
blue cheese / comte / gruyere (optional)
walnuts (optional)
grilled chicken (optional)

Directions

thinly slice the endives and apple
add any optional extras (cheese and walnuts are the original, chicken makes it much heartier)
vinaigrette, mix, done!
