Roasted broccoli with lemon, parmesan, pine nuts

Ingredients

broccoli
olive oil
salt, pepper
parmesan
garlic, sliced
lemons (juiced and zested)
pine nuts
basil (optional)

Directions

preheat oven to 220
cut broccoli into large florets
dry thoroughly
toss with olive oil, salt, pepper, sliced garlic
spread on baking tray
roast for 20-25 minutes, until they are crisp-tender and starting to brown
roast pine nuts
remove broccoli from oven, add lemon zest and juice, more olive oil, roasted pine nuts, parmesan, chopped basil
