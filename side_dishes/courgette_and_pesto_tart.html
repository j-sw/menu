Courgette and pesto tart

Ingredients

2 or 3 large courgettes
Cherry tomatoes (optional)
Garlic
1 sheet of shortcrust pastry
Pesto
Pine nuts (optional)
Olive oil

Directions

Slice up 4 or 5 cloves of garlic
Slice the courgettes into strips lengthwise, brush with olive oil, roast in the oven until soft and golden with some garlic scattered over them
(If pressed for time, you can also fry them in a pan, but the oven is best)
(If out of season and sour, you can scatter a little sugar on them)
Slice the cherry tomatoes in half and squeeze out their juice so you only have the flesh (8 or 9 should do, enough to cover the tart)
Blind bake (i.e. with nothing in it) the shortcrust pastry in a tart tray for 4 or 5 minutes at ~180. The bottom should start to look white and flaky.
Take the tart out of the oven
Spread pesto evenly across the bottom of the tart
Add a layer of tomatoes, interspersed with garlic
Layer the courgettes on top, interspersing with garlic
Scatter some pine nuts across the top
Bake for a further 10-15 mins, until the pine nuts are toasted and the pastry is golden

For bonus presentation points, you can arrange the courgette strips into a rosette shape
