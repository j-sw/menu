Marjorie's Pate

Ingredients

2 onions (diced)
2 cloves garlic chopped up v finely
6oz (170g) butter
1lb (454g) chicken liver (cleaned and chopped up fairly small)
3 tbsp fresh cream
worcester sauce
salt
pepper
small cube chicken bouillon
2 tbsp brandy

Directions

Fry onions and garlic in 4oz (113g) butter until soft (cook v gently)
Clean liver and chop up fairly small, add
Let cook together until soft (if you overcook it will be OK if a little gritty, but best results have it just cooked)
Put in liquidiser (or use fork)
Add cream, salt, pepper, dash Worcester sauce
Melt and stir together 2oz (57g) butter & brandy, add and mix
Put in fridge until hard
