Greek eggplant dish a la Elsie

Ingredients

One large eggplant
2 onions
7 or 8 tomatoes or 2 good cans of tomatoes
One block of fetta
Olive oil
Salt
Pepper
Herbes de Provence
Garlic
Red wine

Directions

Sauce
Heat the garlic in some oil
Brown the diced onions
Add canned tomatoes, or tomatoes that have been cooked to sauce like liquid
Add wine and herbs and salt

Peel the eggplant and cut it into strips
cook in the oven for 20 minutes with olive oil and salt, turning over at 10 minutes. Make sure the strips do not overlap.

Dish
Once the egg plant is soft, use the eggplant to cover the bottom of a dish
Pour on the sauce
Crumble up the fetta on top
Cook for another 10 to 15 minutes
