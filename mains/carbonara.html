Carbonara

Ingredients

eggs (2 per person)
pancetta (should be guanciale, but good luck finding it...)
parmesan (should be pecorino, but...)
spaghetti
salt
pepper

Directions

start the spaghetti going
meanwhile, fry the pancetta until crispy, with a lot of black pepper, drain the excess oil once done
in a bowl, whisk the egg yolks with grated parmesan and more black pepper
once the pasta is done, pour some of the water into the bowl with eggs and mix (the more you add, the more 'safety' you have with the last step), and drain
in the pan with the cooked pancetta, combine pasta and egg mixture, and on very low heat mix through until you obtain a creamy texture
be very careful with the heat - it's easy to end up with scrambled eggs - on an electric stove the residual heat from earlier cooking is enough
serve!
