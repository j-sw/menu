Chicken katsudon

Ingredients

Chicken tenderloins
Egg
Salt + pepper
Breadcrumbs

1 Leek
3 eggs
~80 ml dashi
2 tablespoons light soy sauce
2 teaspoons sugar
1 tablespoon mirin

Rice
Pickled ginger
Japanese Mayo
Tonkatsu sauce
Spring onion (Optional)

Directions

Bread the chicken in seasoned egg + breadcrumbs
Fry
Slice into strips

Mix the dashi, soy, sugar and mirin
Whisk the three eggs together
Using a little bit of oil, fry the leek until soft
Add the dashi sauce mixture and ‘nestle’ the chicken strips into the leek (bring to the boil before adding chicken?)
Stir through / cook while covered until the chicken is saturated with sauce
Add the eggs, cover and cook until eggs are about 80% done

Serve onto rice, with pickled ginger as a garnish (and sliced spring onion if you like)
Add tonkatsu sauce and mayo if desired
