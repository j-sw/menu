Impossible Quiche

Ingredients

3 eggs
salt / pepper
1 cup milk (or 1/2 milk, 1/2 cream)
1/2 cup self raising flour
60g butter
fillings (e.g. leek, bacon, onion, mushrooms, ham, salmon, parsley, tomato)
cheese

Directions

mix base, pour into shallow baking pan
add fillings
top with cheese
bake at 180 for ~1 hour
check if cooked with a knife (should come out clean)
