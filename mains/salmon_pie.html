Salmon pie

Ingredients

~300g salmon
2 decent sized leeks, chopped
~100g baby spinach, blanched (optional, but makes it much lighter)
butter
2 sheets shortcrust pastry
~200ml cream
1 egg
tarragon (main flavour, add more than the rest - as an alternative, can use a lot of chopped flat parsley)
honey (just enough to counteract the bitterness of the leek)
dash of nutmeg (enhances savoury flavour)
salt
pepper

Directions

pre heat oven
blind bake one sheet of shortcrust on a baking tray
blanch the spinach (place in a colander and pour a kettle of boiling water over it), then drain and chop
in a pan, melt butter, fry leeks until softened
add salmon, fry until done
add cream, spinach, tarragon, honey, nutmeg, salt, pepper
cook on low until thickened, breaking up and mixing the salmon and spinach
take off heat and add the egg, mix through
layer onto the baked sheet of pastry, add the second on top
glaze with leftover egg, cream, milk, etc
bake until golden
