Frijoles Charros

Ingredients

500g pinto beans
500g black / kidney beans (optional, but they will add texture while the pinto beans dissolve)
onion to taste, chopped
carrot to taste, chopped
celery to taste, chopped
garlic cloves to taste, whole
pork belly (optional, but adds a lot of flavour)
meats (bacon, chorizo, nduja, etc)
tomatoes, chopped
1/2 onion
chillis, chopped
garlic, chopped
coriander

Directions

if using pork belly, season, grill / roast, chop into smallish chunks - can also do this while the rest is simmering and add part way
in a large pot add beans, onion, carrot, celery, whole garlic cloves, pork belly
add water, about 5cm above ingredients, top up as necessary
cover and simmer for at least 2 hours (longer cooking = creamier beans)
once texture is as wanted:
in a separate pan, fry meats (bacon / chorizo) ~10 minutes
add 1/2 onion, chopped garlic, chilis, soften for ~2 minutes
add tomatoes, cook for ~8 minutes until softened
combine with bean soup, stir through for ~15 minutes
salt to taste - should be rich, salty, spicy, and slightly fresh / sour from the tomatoes
add coriander before serving
tasty by itself, with rice, with bread, on nachos, as a filling for empanada, etc etc
