Orange chicken & potatoes

Ingredients

whole chicken
potatoes, diced
red peppers, diced
1/2 cup olive oil
1/2 cup orange juice
1/2 cup white wine
salt
pepper
orange zest
2 tbsp mild mustard
oregano
thyme
rosemary

Directions

pre heat oven at 180
in a roasting tray, cover chicken with foil, surround with potatoes and peppers
add everything else as the sauce
cook until T-20, while basting, then uncover
