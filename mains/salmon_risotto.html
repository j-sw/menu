Salmon risotto

Ingredients

salmon
risotto rice
leeks, finely chopped
onion, large chunks
carrot, large chunks
celery, large chunks
white wine
butter
olive oil
salt
pepper
parmesan
lemon juice
mushrooms, thinly sliced (optional)
garlic (optional)

Directions

for the stock, chop the onion, carrot, and celery roughly, add to a pot, cover with water, and simmer for ~1 hour
remove the veggies, add the salmon and leave for ~10-15 minutes to cook through
remove salmon and set aside
season the stock to taste, and leave simmering to reduce
if adding mushrooms, melt butter in a pan, add mushrooms, garlic, white wine, cover until the mushrooms have softened / lost their water, then uncover and fry until golden brown
in a large pan, melt butter and olive oil, add the leek, cook until softened
add rice to cover the bottom of the pan, stir for a few minutes until it picks up the fats (slightly translucent on the edges)
add white wine and start stirring (and keep stirring until the end)
as the rice absorbs the liquid, add more stock and keep stirring (once you run out of stock, substitute with boiling water)
once the rice is almost done, add the salmon (and mushrooms, if using)
if you want a bit of texture, can leave some of the green parts of the leek aside until this moment, and add them now
keep going until rice is done, adjusting seasoning if necessary (bearing in mind the parmesan will add more salt)
once rice is done, take off the heat and stir through a generous amount of parmesan
serve with a drizzle of lemon juice
