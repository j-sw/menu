Tofu Caponata

Ingredients

firm tofu
peppers / capsicum, chopped
red onion, chopped
celery, chopped
tinned or ripe tomatoes, chopped
garlic
capers
olive oil
white wine vinegar
sugar
cornflour
tomato paste (optional)
red pepper paste (optional)
green olives, chopped (optional)
chillis (optional)

Directions

squeeze water out of tofu
dice into small cubes
toss in seasoned (salt, pepper) cornflour to coat - will make it crispy
roast until browned (~20 mins at 180)
fry onions until soft, add garlic, cook until fragrant
add tomatoes, white wine, tomato paste, red pepper paste, soften for ~10 minutes
add tofu
add celery, peppers, capers, olives, chillis, stir for a few minutes
add roasted tofu
season with salt, pepper, vinegar, sugar, to taste - you want lightly sweet and lightly sour
cook on low for ~15 minutes (add water if needed)
serve
