Eggplant with pork

Ingredients

asian eggplants (the thin ones)
salt
sugar
2 tbsp oil
minced pork
doubanjiang (spicy broad bean paste)
garlic, finely cut
ginger, finely cut or grated
green onion (cut small, separate whites from greens)
light soy sauce
sesame oil

Directions

cut eggplant into small cubes
soak in a pot with water and salt ~10 minutes, drain
heat oil, fry eggplant until soft / browned
remove eggplant, leave oil
add pork, fry until aromatic / slightly golden brown
move pork to edges of wok, fry doubanjian on low ~1 min
add garlic, ginger, onion whites, cook on low until fragrant
return eggplant, soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, mix and heat
serve on rice with garnish of the green part of the onions
